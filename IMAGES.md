# Images

The photos in `src/assets/` were generated with AI for this fictional
site. They do not show real places, people or brands.

- **Model:** Google Gemini (image generation), October 2026
- **Prompts:** one shared style description plus one prompt per image,
  kept in the maintainer's brief for this template
- **Processing:** cropped to their aspect ratio and scaled to 1600 px
  wide; the build turns them into WebP files in four widths
- **Licence:** like the rest of this repository, the images are under the
  [MIT licence](LICENSE). You may use, change and redistribute them,
  including in your own site.

Images from Gemini carry Google's invisible SynthID watermark, which marks
them as AI-generated.

The logo and favicon are SVG drawings made for this site, under the same
licence. The fonts Geist and Instrument Serif are under the SIL Open Font
License and come from [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org) packages.
